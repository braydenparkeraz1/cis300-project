FAQ

Questions about the service and what is included.

Why is there a yearly charge?

The Safeguards Rule requires your firm to keep its security program current, and your plan commits to a full review at least every 12 months. The yearly plan covers that cycle: reminders, editing, regeneration, and acknowledgments for new staff.

When am I charged?

Nothing is charged while you answer the questionnaire. You pay $400 when you generate your plan, and the $100 yearly renewal begins one year later.

Do I have to renew?

No. The $100 renewal is automatic, but you can cancel it, and you keep every published version to view and export. You will still need to review and update the plan each year, so you would do that outside WISPer.

Is it priced per employee or per office?

No. One firm, one plan, one price, whether you work alone or have several offices. Every employee can sign an acknowledgment at no extra cost.

What if my firm changes during the year?

Update your answers and regenerate as often as you need. Each published version is saved with its date, and paragraphs you edited carry forward.
