Who it’s for

Built for the practice without an IT department

Most tax offices are a preparer or two, a few seasonal staff, and a shared drive. The Safeguards Rule still applies to them, but nobody on staff writes security policy for a living.

WISPer is written for sole practitioners and small CPA firms, where the person answering the questionnaire is usually the same person who prepares the returns. Every question is in plain English, and every answer turns into a section of your plan. Bookkeeping, payroll, and advisory firms that don’t prepare returns get a plan without the IRS-specific parts.

Common objections

“The IRS already has a free template.”

It does, and WISPer follows it. But a template leaves the hard part blank: your data inventory, safeguards, vendors, staff, and the breach rules for each state your clients live in. WISPer writes those from your answers.

“My firm is too small to need one.”

Size doesn’t exempt you. The Safeguards Rule covers sole practitioners too. Firms with data on fewer than 5,000 people skip a few specific steps, but not the written plan.

“We aren’t fully compliant yet.”

Most small firms aren’t. Your plan describes what you do today. Each gap against a legal requirement becomes a dated commitment in Appendix F rather than an overstated claim, and best-practice improvements go in a separate recommendations report.

FAQ

What is a WISP?

A Written Information Security Plan describes how your firm protects client data: who is responsible, what risks you face, which safeguards you use, and what you will do after a breach.

Can I see my plan before I pay?

You can finish the whole questionnaire for free, and a review page flags blank or conflicting answers before you check out. Your firm’s plan is generated at checkout. The sample above shows the summary and every section it will include.

Is any of it written by AI?

Four passages are drafted by AI from your answers: the executive summary overview and, in §3, the operations overview, the basis for each risk rating, and the risk summary. Everything else is standard language filled in from your answers. You must review the AI passages before the plan can be published.

Can I edit the plan afterward?

Yes. Edit any paragraph, publish a new version, and export it. Every published version is saved with its date and a view of what changed.
